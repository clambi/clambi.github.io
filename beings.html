The beings in my work are not designed so much as discovered. I rarely begin with a defined character or identity in mind. Instead, through layering, repetition, and intuitive mark-making, a presence gradually emerges. Faces become a site of alchemization where internal states, fragmented memories, and emotional textures are translated into visual form.

These are a collection of beings that emerge from perception, memory, and feeling.
